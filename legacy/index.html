YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

Settings & backup

Everything is stored on this device. Keep a backup when you need one.

Data & notifications

Your projects, tasks, and time sessions are saved in this browser on this device. They do not automatically sync to another device. Export a backup before changing browsers or clearing site data.

Notifications require permission and browser support. iPad web app notifications require iPadOS 16.4 or later and the site added to the Home Screen. This version does not yet schedule timed reminders.

Calendar connection

This version creates standard .ics calendar files and event links. Direct Google Calendar sync requires a Google Cloud project, OAuth consent, and client configuration. Apple's private Calendar data is not available directly to a website; import or subscribe via calendar files instead.
